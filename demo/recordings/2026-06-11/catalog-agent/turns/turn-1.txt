## Where Does Payment Reconciliation Fit?

**⚠️ There's already a `payment-reconciliation-service` in production.** It lives in:

> **billing-payments** → **payment-processing-system** → `payment-reconciliation-service`

It reconciles inbound payment files from banks, card acquirers, and direct debit clearing houses against the billing ledger. It depends on:
- `billing-account-ledger-service` (the double-entry billing ledger)
- `payment-gateway-service`

It sits alongside `payment-fraud-screening-service` in the same system. Owned by **billing-payments-engineering**.

## The Key Question for You

As someone on the **Claims team**, you have two distinct reconciliation scenarios:

1. **Premium payment reconciliation** — already handled by the existing service above, owned by billing-payments-engineering. This is *not your domain*.

2. **Claims payment reconciliation** — matching outbound settlement disbursements (BACS/SEPA/ACH) against bank confirmations. The `claims-payment-system` currently has 5 components but **no reconciliation service**. That's the gap.

If your service is about reconciling **claim settlement payments**, it belongs in:

> **claims** → **claims-payment-system** → `claims-payment-reconciliation-service`

It would naturally depend on `claims-payment-disbursement-service` (which executes the payments) and `claims-settlement-service` (which calculates net payables).

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
**Domain boundaries matter here.** Premium reconciliation (money coming *in*) and claims reconciliation (money going *out*) are fundamentally different flows owned by different teams. Building a second "payment-reconciliation-service" in the claims domain would be correct — they serve different business functions despite the similar name. The existing service in billing-payments reconciles *bank inflows*; yours would reconcile *disbursement confirmations*.

**Talk to billing-payments-engineering first.** Their existing service has patterns for bank file parsing and exception queuing that you'll likely want to reuse or align with, even if your service is in a different domain.
