## Template: `parasol-quarkus-service`

Tagged `recommended` — explicitly designed for Claims, Underwriting, Policy Administration, and Billing domains. Produces a Maven project with REST endpoint, health checks, metrics, and a `catalog-info.yaml`.

### Step 1 — Service Information (all required)

| Parameter | Type | Your Value |
|---|---|---|
| **name** | `string` (kebab-case, `^[a-z][a-z0-9-]*$`) | `claims-payment-reconciliation-service` |
| **description** | `string` | "Reconciles outbound claim settlement and supplier disbursements against bank confirmation files. Detects unmatched, failed, and duplicate payments and feeds results back to reserve and status tracking." |
| **owner** | Entity picker (Group kind) | `group:default/claims-engineering` |
| **system** | Entity picker (System kind) | `system:default/claims-payment-system` |

### Step 2 — Java Configuration

| Parameter | Type | Default | Your Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **groupId** | `string` | `com.parasol` | `com.parasol.claims` |
| **artifactId** | `string` | *(service name)* | `claims-payment-reconciliation-service` |
| **javaPackageName** | `string` | `com.parasol` | `com.parasol.claims.reconciliation` |
| **port** | `number` | `8080` | `8080` (keep default) |

### What the Template Produces

The scaffolder runs two steps:

1. **`fetch:template`** — pulls the Quarkus skeleton (Maven project structure, REST endpoint, health/metrics wiring)
2. **`fetch:template`** — generates a `catalog-info.yaml` with your owner, system, and `stack: java` tag pre-filled

You'll get a ready-to-run Quarkus project registered in the catalog under `claims-payment-system`, owned by `claims-engineering`.

### Why Not the Other Templates?

| Template | Why Not |
|---|---|
| `parasol-python-service` | Claims governance **requires Java/Quarkus** for transactional services. Python is only allowed for ML/analytical workloads. The billing-payments domain got a Python exception for their reconciliation batch job, but you're in the claims domain — no such exception exists |
| `parasol-nodejs-service` | Only allowed for BFF and I/O-bound notification services. Your service is transactional (matching payments, updating reserves) |

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
**The template doesn't wire up Kafka or IBM MQ.** It gives you a REST skeleton with health checks and metrics. You'll need to add the Kafka consumer configuration yourself — specifically the `quarkus-messaging-kafka` extension for consuming claim lifecycle events (`payment.disbursed`, `settlement.approved`, etc.). Per claims governance, Kafka is the right choice here since you're not on the payment-critical path.

**The `catalog-info.yaml` is the key artifact.** It registers your service in the Backstage catalog with the correct `system: claims-payment-system` and `owner: claims-engineering`. You'll want to add `dependsOn` entries for `claims-payment-disbursement-service` and `claims-settlement-service` after scaffolding — those won't be auto-generated, but they'll make the dependency graph accurate in the catalog.

**The `groupId` convention matters.** Existing claims services use `com.parasol` as the base, but scoping to `com.parasol.claims.reconciliation` keeps your package namespace clean and avoids collisions with the 27 other claims components. Check what the `claims-payment-disbursement-service` uses to stay consistent.
