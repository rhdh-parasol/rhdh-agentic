## Primary Integrations (Must Have)

### 1. `claims-payment-disbursement-service` — **your main data source**

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Claims (same team) |
| **What you need** | Outbound payment instructions — payment ID, claim reference, amount, payment method (BACS/SEPA/ACH/cheque/wallet), beneficiary reference, timestamp |
| **Integration pattern** | Kafka consumer on claims event topic (listen for `PAYMENT_DISPATCHED`, `PAYMENT_SUBMITTED` events) + REST call for payment details on demand |
| **Dependency chain** | This service depends on `bank-account-validation-service` and is consumed by `claims-settlement-service` |
| **Tags** | `claims`, `payment`, `finance`, `java`, `rest` |

### 2. `supplier-payment-service` — **second payment source**

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Claims (same team) |
| **What you need** | Supplier payment batches — payments to garages, contractors, medical providers, legal firms. Purchase order references, invoice numbers, batch IDs |
| **Integration pattern** | Kafka consumer (supplier payment batch events) + REST for batch details |
| **Why it matters** | Supplier payments are a separate outbound stream from claimant disbursements. Your reconciliation needs to cover both. |

### 3. `claims-settlement-service` — **claim context**

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Claims (same team) |
| **What you need** | Settlement details — net payable amount, deductibles applied, co-insurance splits, subrogation deductions. Needed to verify the *expected* payment amount matches what was actually disbursed and cleared. |
| **Integration pattern** | REST call by claim reference |
| **Dependency chain** | Depends on `bank-account-validation-service`, `claims-payment-disbursement-service`, and `claims-reserve-calculator` |

### 4. `payment-gateway-service` — **bank settlement confirmations** (cross-domain)

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Billing & Payments (**different team**) |
| **Owner** | `billing-payments-engineering` |
| **What you need** | Bank settlement files / confirmations — BACS/SEPA clearing reports, ACH return files, payment status updates from acquirers. These tell you whether the money actually landed. |
| **Integration pattern** | This is your key cross-domain integration. Discuss with `billing-payments-engineering` whether they expose settlement file data via an internal API or Kafka topic. Note: the existing `payment-reconciliation-service` in their domain already depends on this service — you may be able to consume the same data feed. |
| **PCI-DSS caution** | This service handles card tokenisation and 3DS2. Do NOT request cardholder data — reconcile using `paymentId` and `paymentToken` references only. |

## Published APIs You'll Consume

### 5. **Claims Status API** — `https://api.parasol.com/claims/status/v1`

| Endpoint | Operation | Why you need it |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /claims/{claimReference}` | `getClaimStatus` | Get current claim status to validate reconciliation context (is the claim still OPEN? SETTLED? DENIED?) |
| `GET /claims/{claimReference}/payments` | `getClaimPayments` | Get existing payment history for a claim — detect duplicates and verify your reconciliation matches the canonical payment record |

### 6. **Audit Log API** — `https://api-internal.parasol.com/compliance/audit/v1`

| Endpoint | Operation | Why you need it |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /events` | `submitAuditEvents` | **Mandatory**. You must submit audit events for every reconciliation action — matched payments, exceptions flagged, failed payment investigations. Required fields: `timestamp`, `eventType`, `actorId`, `subjectId`, `resourceType`, `outcome` (SUCCESS/FAILURE/DENIED) |

This is an org-wide compliance requirement, not optional.

## Downstream Consumers (Services that need YOUR output)

### 7. `subrogation-recovery-service`

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Why** | If a claimant payment failed or was returned, subrogation recovery needs to know — the recovery accounting depends on confirmed settlement. Your reconciliation results feed into their recovery calculations. |
| **Pattern** | Emit Kafka events: `PAYMENT_RECONCILED`, `PAYMENT_FAILED`, `PAYMENT_RETURNED` |

### 8. `claim-status-tracker` (in `claims-assessment-system`)

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Why** | When a payment is confirmed cleared, the claim milestone should update. When a payment fails, the claim status may need to revert. |
| **Pattern** | Kafka events on the claims event topic trigger milestone updates |
