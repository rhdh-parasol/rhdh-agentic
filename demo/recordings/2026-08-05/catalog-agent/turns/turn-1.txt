## The Key Finding

There's already a **`payment-reconciliation-service`** — but it lives in the **Billing & Payments** domain under `payment-processing-system`. It reconciles *inbound premium payments* (bank files, card acquirers, direct debit) against the billing ledger.

That service is **not** what you need. It handles the *premium* side. Your Claims team needs *claims settlement* reconciliation — confirming that outbound disbursements (BACS/SEPA/ACH, cheques, wallet transfers) from the `claims-payment-disbursement-service` actually landed in claimant and supplier bank accounts.

## Where Your Service Fits

**Domain:** Claims
**System:** `claims-payment-system` — alongside the existing 5 services:
- `claims-settlement-service` (calculates net payable)
- `claims-payment-disbursement-service` (executes the payment)
- `bank-account-validation-service` (validates accounts pre-payment)
- `supplier-payment-service` (pays garages, contractors, legal firms)
- `subrogation-recovery-service` (recovers from third parties)

Your **claims payment reconciliation service** would slot in as the feedback loop: it confirms disbursements cleared, flags failed/returned payments, and reconciles bank settlement files against outbound payment instructions. It's the missing piece between "we sent the money" and "it arrived."

You'll likely need to consume APIs from:
- **`claims-payment-disbursement-service`** (your own domain — payment instructions and status)
- **`payment-gateway-service`** (Billing & Payments domain — for bank settlement files/confirmations)
