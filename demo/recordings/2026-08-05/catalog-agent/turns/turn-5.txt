#### Cross-Domain Conflict #1: Kafka vs IBM MQ

| Dimension | Claims Domain Says | Billing Domain Says |
|---|---|---|
| **Preferred broker** | AMQ Streams (Kafka) — preferred for all async domain events | IBM MQ — required for payment-critical paths |
| **Rationale** | Event-driven claim lifecycle | Guaranteed delivery; at-least-once Kafka semantics not approved for payment-critical flows |
| **Resolution path** | Your service lives in Claims and emits to the claims event topic via Kafka. But if you *consume* payment settlement data from Billing's `payment-gateway-service`, their rules apply to that integration. The Billing handbook explicitly grants a reconciliation batch exception: if your service does not handle cardholder data, you can request approval to use Kafka instead of IBM MQ. |
| **Action required** | Request exception from `billing-payments-engineering` lead + `parasol-platform-engineering`. Document in ADR. If denied, add IBM MQ client for the cross-domain feed only. |

### PCI-DSS Scope

| Decision | Choice | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PCI-DSS** | **Avoid scope** — reconcile on tokenized payment references only | Billing Handbook — "Components tagged `pci-dss` MUST NOT log, cache, or persist cardholder data (PAN, CVV, expiry) in plaintext" | If you never touch cardholder data, you stay out of PCI-DSS scope and avoid Guardium, PCI-hardened images, and annual QSA audit |

#### Cross-Domain Conflict #2: PCI-DSS Scope Boundary

| Dimension | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Data from `payment-gateway-service`** | This service handles card tokenisation, 3DS2, Worldpay/Stripe integration. Any data feed from it *could* include cardholder data. | Explicitly request only `paymentId`, `paymentToken`, `status`, `amount`, `settlementDate` — never PAN, CVV, or expiry. Document this boundary in your OpenAPI spec and ADR. |
| **If you accidentally enter PCI scope** | Triggers: Guardium database monitoring, PCI-hardened base images, annual on-site QSA audit, quarterly vulnerability scans, Billing domain exception process | This is expensive and slow. Design the integration contract to exclude cardholder fields from day one. |
