# Parasol Insurance Engineering Standards

Cross-cutting engineering standards for all Parasol Insurance Group engineering teams.
These standards apply to all domains and systems unless a domain handbook explicitly
grants an exception.

## Scope

These standards govern:

- **API Design** — conventions derived from Parasol's 16 production APIs
- **Observability** — metrics, logging, and tracing requirements
- **Security & Authentication** — identity, authorization, and data protection patterns

## Compliance

All new services must comply with these standards before production deployment.
Domain handbooks may define stricter requirements but may not relax org-level standards
without Platform Engineering approval.
